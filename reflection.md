- What are your initial impressions of how `Encapsulation` affects your programming?

Encapsulation helps me better understand which classes are in charge of which information and how they interact. It forces me to write appropriate methods for each class, rather than accessing and modifying attributes directly. 

- What worked, what didn't, what advice would you give someone taking this course in the future?

I wrote my methods following the exact order listed in README.md, which worked well because it broke the assignment into manageable pieces. One thing that was tricky was deciding how to print the rosters, especially since the process was split between two different methods. This just took a bit of experimenting until I found a clear, readable format. My advice to future students is to keep in mind the methods you've written for other classes while writing new ones; this helped me remember to call other methods instead of writing redundant code.
